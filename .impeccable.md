# .impeccable.md — ZeroSpendStreak

## Design Context

### Users
20–30대 사회초년생 (young professionals in early careers) who want to build sustainable saving habits but lack daily motivation and accountability. They are app-tech savvy users already familiar with gamification and habit-tracking apps in the Korean fintech ecosystem. The core job: establish daily zero-spend discipline through self-tracking, celebrate streak milestones, and maintain momentum through friendly competition with peers.

### Brand Personality
**Playful, Motivating, Trustworthy.** Tone is encouraging without being preachy—celebrate small daily wins, make streak progression feel rewarding, and foster friendly rivalry rather than aggressive competition. Voice aligns with Toss' approachable yet responsible fintech positioning: simple, clear, and supportive of healthy financial habits.

### Aesthetic Direction
TDS (Toss Design System) 준수. @toss/tds-mobile 컴포넌트 전용. 커스텀 미적 방향 없음.

### UX Principles
1. **Frictionless Daily Check-in** — Make the core habit loop (check in success/failure) instant and rewarding, with minimal steps to reinforce the daily ritual.
2. **Clear Progress Visualization** — Display streaks, weekly/monthly stats, and milestones prominently so users immediately see the impact of their discipline.
3. **Healthy Social Dynamics** — Enable friend leaderboards and invites that encourage motivation through comparison, not shame; emphasize personal progress alongside rank.
4. **Immediate Celebration** — Acknowledge daily wins, badge unlocks, and streak milestones with micro-interactions that reinforce the dopamine loop.
5. **Strategic Ad Placement** — Surface ads as a second-chance recovery tool (after streak failure) rather than interruption, turning monetization into a user benefit.
